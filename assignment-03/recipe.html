How to Prepare Nigerian Jollof Rice

Jollof rice is a popular West African dish prepared with rice, tomatoes, peppers, onions and spices. It is commonly served at parties, weddings and family gatherings in Nigeria.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups of long-grain parboiled rice
  • 4 fresh tomatoes
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 1 scotch bonnet pepper
  • 2 medium onions
  • 3 tablespoons of tomato paste
  • Half a cup of vegetable oil
  • 3 cups of chicken or vegetable stock
  • 1 teaspoon of curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon of dried thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Salt and seasoning to taste

Preparation Steps

  1. Wash the rice thoroughly and drain the water.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, peppers and one onion until smooth.
  3. Heat the vegetable oil in a pot.
  4. Chop the remaining onion and fry it until soft.
  5. Add the tomato paste and fry for about 3 minutes.
  6. Add the blended tomato mixture and cook for 10 to 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  7. Add the stock, curry powder, thyme, bay leaves, salt and seasoning.
  8. Add the washed rice and stir gently.
  9. Cover the pot and cook on low heat for 25 to 35 minutes, adding a little hot water if necessary.
  10. When the rice is tender, remove the bay leaves, turn off the heat and serve.

Safety Notes

Kitchen Safety

Warning: Never pour water into hot oil because it can cause dangerous splashing.

Always use low heat when simmering the rice to prevent burning.

Food Safety

Wash your hands before handling ingredients. Cool leftover rice quickly, ideally within one hour, and refrigerate it promptly.

Cooking Terms and Their Meanings

Simmer
To cook food gently in liquid just below boiling point.
Blend
To combine ingredients into a smooth mixture using a blender.
Saute
To cook food quickly in a small amount of oil.
Season
To add salt, herbs or spices to improve the flavour of food.

Serving Suggestions

Serve the Jollof rice with fried plantain, grilled chicken, fish or a fresh vegetable salad.
